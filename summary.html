Summary

From this analysis, we were able to find that the Orange Line is most delayed during the hours of 6-8 PM. One of our key hypotheses, that passenger flow would heavily impact delays, such as how popular stops like Massachusetts Avenue were more likely to be delayed than Forest Hills were occassionally correct, but not consistent enough to be conclusive. Key attributes leading to delays from our analysis included primarily the stop's position on the line and time. Northbound trains, which head from the beginning of the line at Forest Hills almost always have lesser delays than those heading South Bound. Additionally, the timing of the trains seems to have great impact on the delays, seemingly regardless of passenger flow at the time.

This was a surprising conclusion given our experience with delays at more travelled stops. Looking into the future, it would be relevant and interesting to tackle why these hours of 6-8pm are so much more delayed than the other hours, given no apparent increase in passenger flow and greater increase than later hours, which have accumulated more time to potentially have delays.
